Corporate Coaching

While your team focuses on fulfilling the roles and jobs that you’ve hired them for, you understand there are many pieces to a workplace. It’s a dynamic environment with complex, moving parts. As coaches, we are equipped and ready to come alongside your busy staff to add value in the critical intangibles. Trust, communication, expectations, perceptions and relationships go unnoticed when aligned; yet these same things are the source of massive friction when they are not. We help lock these things in to increase your team’s execution and capacity.

Coaching Setups

Various corporate coaching setups are available, depending on the needs of your organization.

As-Needed

We can be available to your team on an as-needed basis.

Retainer

We can be kept on retainer for regular meetings with varied sets of team members.

Tiered Engagement

The best results come from a tiered, termed engagement where our coaches can meet with individuals and their teams at all levels on a regular basis.

Our coaches each have their own specific tools and methods for success. The lion’s share of our corporate coaching is built on the proven framework of GiANT.

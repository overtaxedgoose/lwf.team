Joel Lam

Hi, I am Joel, the Founder of The Breakthrough People. It is my personal belief that personal renewal precedes corporate renewal, and the clarity of one’s own journey will give rise to a church with greater influence in all spheres of society.

My passion for people and leadership development became apparent after I attended a Leadership Course in 2003 called Focused Living. I was inspired by Dr. Robert Clinton (Professor Emeritus of Leadership from 1981 - 2016 at Fuller Theological Seminary), who cited that only one out of three leaders finishes well. Those who do, make the concerted decision to prioritise personal development. I was challenged to confront this statistic and influence change.

Having coached and trained leaders in the different spheres for the past 15 years, I am further compelled to improve the statistic and help more everyday leaders stay on course and finish well!

Keith W. Currie

A native of Tennessee, Keith earned his B.S. from Trevecca Nazarene College, Nashville, TN, and his M.S. from George Peabody College for Teachers, Vanderbilt University.

As an educator, Keith spent four decades in public and private Christian schools. As head of school for over three decades, Keith sharpened his individual and team development skills. His leadership was characterized by high employee retention, a culture of peace, and students who understand, communicate, and influence. Those who served with him consider him a Leader Worth Following.

Today, Keith partners with GiANT, the worldwide leader in leadership and team performance for the 21st Century.

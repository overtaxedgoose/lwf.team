Who are we?

Keith and Trish provide schools with the practical tools to empower the leadership and staﬀ, to strengthen their mission, and to cultivate a sustainable legacy of academic and spiritual growth.

Keith Currie

Keith has his BS from Trevecca Nazarene University and his MS from George Peabody College for Teachers (Vanderbilt). He taught seven years as a secondary classroom teacher in the public school systems in Tennessee and in Alabama. In 1984, he became the principal of the start-up of Covenant Christian School, Mobile, AL, where he served for thirty-eight years. Local high schools competed for the 8th grade graduates from Covenant Christian because of their leadership and academic preparedness.

Keith remains involved in education through the Herzog Foundation and American Association of Christian Schools as a mentor for Christian school start-ups. He also serves established schools in areas of administration, leadership, succession, teacher and team collaboration, and staff development.

Trish Currie

Trish has her BS from San Francisco State with graduate studies from University of South Alabama, and is certified in the Otis-Gillingham Approach to language and reading.

For twenty-nine years, Trish taught in public and private schools in California and Alabama, elementary and middle schools including administrative duties. An excellent classroom teacher and middle school language arts teacher, her students returned often to thank her for preparing them for the rigors of high school. Trish is a trained life/leadership coach and continues to mentor teachers, develop staff and faculty collaboration. She has also served in a counseling role in a new micro-school start-up.

LWF Around the World

South Asia 2026

Melody and Adelaide are going to be heading to South Asia this summer. This will be a first for both of them to visit this area in Asia, and Adelaide’s first country outside of Canada & the US. They will be joining another mother/daughter duo who lived with their family in this area for more than a decade. The four of them will visit the families this duo knows and still works with on a regular basis. Their focus is mainly creating jobs for women using a business as mission model. They also help with young people through tutoring and scholarships. This has been a massive game changer for these ladies and their families.

Your Opportunity to Be Involved

You’re invited to participate through giving financially to their trip expenses. This doesn’t just have to be an experience for Adelaide & Melody; not everyone can make these journeys. Through giving, you are part of the team sending them and supporting the families in this region. You can play a part in this endeavor.
